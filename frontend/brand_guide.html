3. Logo Usage (قواعد الاستخدام)

Clear Space (مساحة الأمان)

Always leave a padding of at least 25% of the logo's width around it. Do not crowd the logo with other elements or text.

Do's and Don'ts (المسموح والممنوع)

✓ DO: Use on dark backgrounds

The logo is optimized for Zinc-950 (#09090B) or pure black. It glows best in dark mode.

✕ DON'T: Stretch or distort

Never alter the aspect ratio. The precise geometric angles must remain intact.

✕ DON'T: Change gradient colors

Do not re-color the logo to red, green, etc. Stick to the official brand blues.

✓ DO: Use white/monochrome

When placed on busy photography, you may use a pure white flat version of the logo.

6. Design Elements & Social Media (عناصر التصميم)

LinkedIn Carousels & Post Backgrounds

When designing for LinkedIn or Behance:

  • Use #09090B (Zinc 950) as your solid background canvas.
  • Add subtle blurred radial gradients of #38BDF8 (Opacity 10-15%) behind key mockup elements to create depth.
  • Ensure text contrast by using #F8FAFC (Slate 50) for main text and #94A3B8 (Slate 400) for secondary text.

Glassmorphism (تأثير الزجاج)

For cards, UI elements, and overlapping layers in your mockups, use this exact CSS/Figma specification:

Figma Settings:
Fill: #141414 (60%) | Background Blur: 16 | Stroke: #FFFFFF (10%)
